## CHAPTER 10
## CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

### 10.1   Conclusion

Q Yaar effectively addresses the critical need for a secure, privacy-preserving, and instant communication channel between vehicle owners and the general public. The platform combines user-friendly design with powerful features like QR code-based vehicle identification, real-time chat powered by Socket.io, automated push notifications via Expo, and robust anti-spam mechanisms including message rate limiting and session auto-expiry. This provides a structured, secure, and scalable solution for both vehicle owners and scanners to connect and communicate without exposing personal contact details. The system enhances urban mobility, parking dispute resolution, and emergency communication, helping users resolve vehicle-related situations efficiently. By integrating modern technologies and attending to user privacy and safety needs, Q Yaar stands as a comprehensive vehicle communication platform with significant potential impact on urban life.

### 10.2   Future Work

In the future, the platform can be strengthened by incorporating AI-driven license plate recognition to enable automatic vehicle identification through camera scanning, eliminating the need for physical QR codes in certain scenarios. The system can also introduce location-based services, allowing vehicle owners to receive alerts when their parked vehicle is in a tow-away zone or restricted parking area, supported by automated SMS and push reminders. To expand its impact, the platform may collaborate with parking management companies, housing societies, and traffic police departments to offer large-scale, managed vehicle communication programs. Additionally, extending compatibility to wearable devices such as smartwatches, along with integrating multilingual support and voice-based messaging features, will help make the platform more inclusive and widely usable. Engagement can be further improved through gamification elements such as responsible parking badges, community trust scores, and reward points for positive interactions. Finally, security can be enhanced by implementing end-to-end encryption for all chat messages, biometric authentication for app login, and blockchain-based QR verification to prevent QR code tampering and cloning.
